Clear expectations for your property media.

How do panoramic walkthroughs work?

Visitors look around each room and move between linked panoramic viewpoints. This is not a measured 3D model and includes no dollhouse view, floor plans, or automatic Zillow integration.

What does photography include?

25–40 edited interior and exterior images, depending on home square footage. Sample tiers run from $179 to $259 through 5,000 sq ft.

How do bundle savings work?

Two or more different services at the same property and appointment receive 15% off the service subtotal. One service receives no discount. No discount on travel, tax, hosting extensions, or custom charges; promotions do not stack.

What about larger properties?

Enter your square footage for sample pricing through 5,000 sq ft. Larger homes, parcels above two acres, extra panoramas, and complex projects need a custom quote. Land requests start with aerial services.

How long is tour hosting included?

Six months. Hosting extensions are quoted before that period ends. A real sample tour and hosting provider must be confirmed before launch.

What about weather and flight availability?

Aerial work depends on suitable weather, safe conditions, and flight availability. Scheduling and any changes are confirmed manually.

When will media arrive?

Targets: photos within two business days; tours and video within three business days, pending operational confirmation. Timing is confirmed for your project.

Are branded and unbranded options available?

Discuss branded and unbranded delivery options when confirming the project.

Are boundary overlays surveys?

Any boundary overlays are illustrative, based on client-supplied information, and are not surveys.

When will the media be delivered?

You choose your requested media delivery date. Rey reviews the deadline and confirms it with you, then schedules the capture separately. The date is not a capture appointment or a delivery guarantee.
